What is the difference between a horizontal and a vertical navigation menu?

A horizontal navigation menu displays links in a row, usually across the top of a website. A vertical navigation menu displays links in a column, one below another, usually along the side of a website.

Question
What do justify-content and flex-wrap each control?

Justify-content controls how items are aligned and spaced along the main axis of a flex container, flex-wrap controls whether items stay on one line or move onto multiple lines when there is not enough space.

Question
What is the difference between position: relative and position: absolute in your submenu?

[position: relative] positions an element relative to its normal position and keeps its space in the layout. [position: absolute] positions an element relative to its nearest positioned ancestor and removes it from the normal layout flow.
