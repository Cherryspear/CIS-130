Preparing for a Hike

Good preparation can make a hiking trip safer and more enjoyable. Beginners should choose a trail that matches their experience and fitness level.

Things to Bring

  1. Water
  2. Comfortable hiking shoes
  3. Weather-appropriate clothing
  4. A small first-aid kit
  5. A map or trail guide

Hiking Safely

Stay on marked trails and pay attention to signs along the way. It is also a good idea to tell someone where you are going before starting your hike.

Always check the weather before leaving and bring enough water for the length of your trip.

Beginner Tip

Start small. A short, easy trail can help you learn what equipment you need before attempting a longer hike.
